On-Device AI Engine

How WhisperType transcribes speech entirely on your Mac.

Unlike cloud speech recognition services that stream your voice recording across the web to remote servers, WhisperType operates 100% locally. Using OpenAI's Whisper model compiled to Apple CoreML, it processes audio directly on your Apple Silicon Neural Engine.

🎙️ In-Memory Audio CaptureRAM Only

When you hold or tap ⌥Space, AVAudioEngine buffers 16 kHz mono PCM audio directly into memory. Audio is never written to disk or recorded to temporary files, ensuring complete security.

⚡ Apple Neural Engine InferenceCoreML

Audio frames are passed straight to WhisperKit CoreML running on your M-series Neural Engine and GPU. Transcriptions finish in milliseconds with near-zero battery drain.

⌨️ Seamless Text InsertionAccessibility

WhisperType synthesizes a native paste keystroke (⌘V) into whichever app is currently focused. It saves and restores your prior clipboard contents so your copied text is never lost.

🗂️ 25-Item Dictation HistoryQuick Copy

Misplaced a dictated note? Open the Studio dashboard to view your last 25 transcriptions. Each entry includes timestamps and a one-click copy button to retrieve text anytime.

🧠 Memory ManagementRAM Control

Keep your active model pinned in unified memory for instant hotkey response, or use the one-click Unload Model option in the Control Panel to free system RAM instantly when not dictating.

⏱️ Smart Silence DetectionAuto-Stop

Configure auto-stop between 1 and 10 seconds of silence. WhisperType detects when you stop talking and automatically finalizes and inserts your words without requiring a keypress.
