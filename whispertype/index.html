From speech to clean writing

Speak the way you think. WhisperType cleans it up.

Human speech is messy — filled with "um"s, false starts, and mid-sentence corrections. Whisper parses natural cadence, strips verbal clutter, and inserts proper punctuation.

Team Slack / Chat
What you said (raw speech) "hey um so like I was thinking we should push the release to like friday afternoon coz the build is failing... actually wait make it monday morning"
What WhisperType typed "I was thinking we should push the release to Monday morning because the build is failing."
Technical / Professional Notes
What you said (raw speech) "patient presenting with acute rhinitis prescribing amoxicillin five hundred milligrams b i d for ten days follow up if fever persists"
What WhisperType typed "Patient presenting with acute rhinitis. Prescribing amoxicillin 500 mg BID for 10 days. Follow up if fever persists."

On-device Whisper

OpenAI's Whisper models run locally on the Apple Neural Engine — accurate with pauses, accents and technical words, and fast on Apple Silicon.

Types into any field

The transcription is pasted into the focused app, then your previous clipboard is restored — dictation never quietly overwrites what you copied.

Floating HUD

A borderless meter breezes in under the menu bar so you can see your voice being picked up, then switches to a spinner while it transcribes.

Auto-stop on silence

Stop talking and WhisperType stops on its own after a timeout you set (1–10s). Or press esc anytime to throw the audio away without inserting.

Pick your model

From Tiny for quick notes to Medium for long-form accuracy — swap models anytime and manage what's on disk from the Storage panel.

Push-to-talk & audio cues

Tap to start and stop, or hold to speak like a walkie-talkie. A subtle chime confirms text landed without pulling your eyes off your work.

Studio Dashboard

A dedicated floating Studio window to monitor microphone input levels, switch Whisper models on the fly, and view live transcription status.

25-Item Dictation History

Never lose a thought. Access your 25 most recent transcriptions in the Studio dashboard with timestamps and instant one-click copy icons.

Memory & RAM Control

Keep your model warm in unified memory for zero-lag hotkey triggering, or click Unload Model in the Control Panel to immediately free system RAM.

Never saved to disk

Your speech lives in RAM only while transcribing and disappears the moment it's typed. A discreet menu bar dot shows when the mic is listening.
