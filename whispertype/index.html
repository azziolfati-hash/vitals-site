The part other dictation tools get wrong

It types where you're already typing.

No separate window to copy out of. When a transcription is ready, WhisperType puts it into whatever field currently has focus — then quietly restores your clipboard so nothing you copied is lost.

  • ✓Inserts into any app — Notes, Mail, Safari, iMessage, Slack, your editor.
  • ✓Copies the text, pastes it with ⌘V, then restores your previous clipboard.
  • ✓Prefer to paste yourself? Turn auto-paste off — the text just waits on your clipboard.
  • ✓Press esc while recording or transcribing to cancel and insert nothing.

The AI model lives on your Mac.

WhisperType downloads a Whisper model once, then runs it entirely offline on the GPU and Neural Engine — so transcription is fast, private, and works on a plane. Pick the size that fits your Mac and your work.

  • ✓Choose from Tiny → Large — trade speed for accuracy, and switch anytime.
  • ✓Runs on the Apple Neural Engine, keeping the CPU cool and the battery happy.
  • ✓One-time download; after that everything works fully offline.
  • ✓See what's on disk and reclaim space from the Storage panel.
