Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: WhisperType collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours in the loop. Your voice is captured in memory, transcribed on your Mac's own Neural Engine, and never written to disk or sent anywhere. This page explains exactly what that means.

What happens to your audio

When you dictate, WhisperType captures microphone audio into memory, converts it to text on-device using a Whisper model running on the Apple Neural Engine, and then discards the audio. Nothing is ever written to disk, uploaded, or synced. The transcription is placed into the app you're using (and on your clipboard) and lives only where you put it.

The transcription, and your clipboard

  • ✓The text is inserted into whatever field currently has focus, exactly as if you'd typed it — it stays in that app, on your Mac.
  • ✓To paste, WhisperType briefly uses the clipboard, then restores your previous clipboard contents — your copied data isn't kept or sent.
  • ✓Prefer to paste yourself? Turn off auto-paste and the text simply waits on your clipboard for a manual ⌘V.

What's stored, and where

  • ✓Preferences (your hotkey, model choice, and options) are kept in standard macOS app preferences, local to your Mac.
  • ✓The AI model files are downloaded once into your Documents folder (~/Documents/huggingface/…) and used offline from then on.
  • ✓Your purchase is validated through the App Store / StoreKit under your own Apple ID. WhisperType stores no payment details.

The one thing that touches the network

The first time you use a model you don't have yet, WhisperType downloads it from the public Hugging Face repository argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml. That download contains no personal data — it only fetches the model files. After it completes, transcription works entirely offline. If you ever choose "Report a bug" inside the app, that report is sent only when you explicitly tap Send — see below.

If you report a bug

WhisperType includes an optional, user-initiated bug reporter. Nothing is ever sent in the background — only when you tap "Send Report." A basic report contains your message, the app and macOS version, and an email only if you type one. You can optionally include an anonymous diagnostics profile (Mac model, CPU/RAM, locale, uptime, thermal state); it contains no name, account, files, location, or any identifier that ties two reports together, and you can inspect the exact contents before sending.

The permissions WhisperType asks for

WhisperType asks for Microphone access so it can hear your dictation, and Accessibility access so it can paste the text into the focused field and let esc cancel from other apps. Both are macOS platform permissions you grant in System Settings, and both are local — they let WhisperType do its job on your Mac and send nothing anywhere.

Children's privacy

WhisperType is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — there's simply nothing collected, full stop.
