Buying & Licensing

Buying, activating, and moving your WhisperType license

Everything about the direct purchase: how your license key works, where to enter it, how to move it to a new Mac, and how the 3-device limit covers all your hardware.

No Subscriptions. Own it Forever.

WhisperType is a single $19.99 one-time payment. There are no monthly fees, no recurring cloud tokens, and no subscriptions. Every update and model enhancement is included.

Try It Free for 14 Days

WhisperType starts a 14-day free trial automatically upon initial launch. Every feature — all four local models (Tiny, Base, Small, Medium), 25-item dictation history, custom hotkeys, and silence detection — is fully unlocked. No credit card or account is required to start.

Activating Your License Key

  1. Purchase WhisperType from store.hanncrest.com.
  2. Your receipt email arrives immediately with your unique license key.
  3. Open WhisperType, open the Settings / Control Panel, and navigate to the License tab.
  4. Paste your key and click Activate. Your status updates to "WhisperType Licensed" permanently.
Offline Tolerance: WhisperType validates your key with Lemon Squeezy once upon activation. Afterward, it verifies silently at launch. If you're offline or traveling without Wi-Fi, WhisperType fails open so you are never locked out of dictation.

Why the 3-Device Limit Works in Your Favor

A single $19.99 license key entitles you to activate WhisperType on up to three personal Macs simultaneously (for example, your MacBook Air, work laptop, and desktop Mac Studio). Need to swap machines? Simply click "Deactivate on this Mac…" under Settings ▸ License, and that activation slot frees up immediately.

Can I move my license to a new Mac?

Yes. Simply click "Deactivate on this Mac" in Settings ▸ License on your older Mac. If you no longer have access to the machine, you can deactivate any instance from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal linked in your order receipt.

Do I need an internet connection to dictate?

No. You only need internet connection once to download your chosen Whisper model file. Once downloaded, all speech transcription runs 100% locally on your Mac's Apple Silicon Neural Engine.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

When the trial period ends, dictation is temporarily paused until you enter a valid license key. None of your downloaded models or settings are deleted.
