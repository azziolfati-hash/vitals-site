Common questions

How do I start dictating?

Press ⌥ Space (Option-Space) anywhere on your Mac to start, and press it again to stop and insert. Prefer to hold? Switch to push-to-talk in the Control Panel and hold the hotkey while you speak. You can change the hotkey, and set a separate stop key, in the Control Panel.

Which permissions does WhisperType need, and why?

Microphone is required so WhisperType can hear your dictation. Accessibility is required to paste the transcription into the focused field (and for esc-to-cancel while another app is in front). Grant both under System Settings → Privacy & Security. The Control Panel shows the live status of each with one-click buttons to the right settings pane.

It transcribes, but won't auto-paste after an update — how do I fix it?

macOS sometimes invalidates the Accessibility grant when an app's signature changes (common right after an update). Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, select WhisperType, click the – (minus) to remove it, then dictate again so macOS re-prompts. Your transcription always stays on the clipboard, so you can paste manually with ⌘V in the meantime.

Which model should I choose?

Small is the default and the best balance of speed and accuracy for most Macs. Choose Tiny or Base for quick notes and lower-RAM Macs, or Large / Large Turbo for long-form and technical dictation (Turbo is nearly as accurate as Large but much faster, and multilingual). You can switch anytime and manage what's on disk in the Storage panel.

Does my Mac need to be online? Is my voice sent anywhere?

The only time WhisperType uses the network is the one-time model download. After that it works fully offline. Your audio is transcribed on-device on the Apple Neural Engine and is never written to disk or sent anywhere — there's no account and no analytics.

My Mac has no built-in microphone — can I still use it?

Yes — connect any external mic, a USB headset, or use an iPhone via Continuity. Macs without a built-in mic (Mac mini, Mac Studio, Mac Pro) just need an input device available; the Control Panel will show when a microphone is detected.
