The part drag-to-Trash gets wrong

A real uninstaller.

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves its preferences, caches, containers and login items behind — quietly eating gigabytes for apps you thought were gone. Breeze finds all of it and removes it together.

  • ✓Finds every copy of an app on disk, not just the one in /Applications.
  • ✓Sweeps preferences, caches, containers, login items, launch agents and receipts.
  • ✓Catches hidden data folders apps create outside the usual Library locations.
  • ✓Anything it can't be certain about is held back for review — never auto-selected.

For developers & creators

The 50–100 GB power users hoard.

Safe by design

Scan → Review → Clean.

Breeze never deletes on a hunch. Every cleanup shows you exactly what it found, and everything it removes goes to the Trash — so you can always get it back.

STEP 1

Scan

A read-only pass across caches, logs, leftovers, duplicates and large files — with real sizes, nothing touched yet.

STEP 2

Review
