Everything you want to know

One glance. The whole machine.

From a tidy menu-bar readout to a deep, chart-rich dashboard — Vitals shows you what's happening and what it means.

Menu-bar vitals

Just the Vitals mark by default — clean and unobtrusive. Add live CPU, GPU, RAM & network readouts with mini-graphs whenever you want. Click for a rich popover snapshot.

Local-LLM tools

Auto-detects Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp & more on any port. Runs real tokens/sec speed tests and shows VRAM headroom.

30-day history

Every metric charted over a month, searchable — with weekly reports you can export to CSV.

Heat, fans & health

Per-component temperatures, fan speeds, thermal throttling detection and S.M.A.R.T. disk health.

Crash & panic watch

Surfaces app crashes and kernel panics with a plain-English explanation of what actually happened.

Live desktop widget

A floating, always-on-top panel that updates every second — not on a slow schedule. Drag it anywhere, expand it to the full dashboard, or jump to the overview window in one click.

Bluetooth & peripherals

Battery level for your connected keyboard, mouse, trackpad and headphones, right alongside your Mac's own battery.

Battery vampires

Catches a single app quietly pinning the CPU and draining your charge — on battery, or always, if you turn that on. Tame it, Freeze it (and Resume later, nothing lost), or Force Quit it in one tap.

Sleep drain report

Every sleep and wake, tracked — plus the battery it cost you and what was still holding a wake lock right as the lid closed. The answer to the backpack-hot, battery-drained mystery.

Smart alerts

Customizable threshold alerts, with an optional push to your iPhone the moment something needs attention.

Deep customization

Twelve themes, full menu-bar layout control, and a live desktop widget that matches how you like to work.

Every core named right

Reads Apple Silicon's true performance tiers (hw.perflevelN) — correctly labeling Super, Performance and Efficiency cores instead of guessing. Includes a throttling timeline that points to the exact app causing it.

Network lab & Wi-Fi

Real-time latency, jitter, packet loss, DNS benchmarks, rolling Wi-Fi signal/noise history, and monthly data-cap tracking so nothing sneaks past you.

Storage & SSD wear

Keeps a running tally of bytes written, S.M.A.R.T. health ratings, read/write speeds, and which apps are writing heavily to your drive.

Local-LLM tools

Built for the local-AI era.

Running models on your own Mac? Vitals finds every local inference server automatically — named or not, on any port — and tells you what's actually running and how fast.

  • ✓Detects Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, vLLM, Jan, GPT4All, KoboldCpp and more.

The Architecture of Privacy

What Vitals does NOT do.

Most monitoring apps ask for your email, phone home on startup, or upload telemetry. Vitals operates under a strict zero-knowledge design.

No Telemetry or Tracking

Zero analytics SDKs. No Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Firebase, and no Sentry telemetry. We do not know who you are, what Mac you own, or what apps you run.

No Account or Registration

No sign-in, no passwords, and no email newsletter signups required. Download, double-click, and start monitoring immediately. Pro unlocks with a standard App Store purchase or license, verified on your Mac.

No Background Network Traffic

Vitals reads your Mac locally via IOKit, sysctl, and SMC, and makes no network calls in the background. A few opt-in Pro tools — the speed test, DNS benchmark, public-IP check, and phone-push alerts — do connect out, but only when you run them. You can independently verify this using Little Snitch, LuLu, or Wireshark.

Compatibility & System Requirements

Built for modern macOS.

Universal 2 binary running natively on macOS 13 Ventura, Sonoma, Sequoia, and macOS 26 Tahoe. Here is how features adapt to your hardware:

Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4)

Full hardware-level feature set unlocked via native Apple Silicon APIs:

  • ✓True core cluster labeling: Uses hw.perflevelN to accurately label Super, Performance, and Efficiency cores.
  • ✓Thermal throttling timeline: Direct hardware event attribution pointing to the exact app causing thermal throttling.
  • ✓Local LLM & Unified Memory: Live VRAM headroom and tokens/second benchmarks for Ollama / LM Studio.

Intel Macs

Comprehensive monitoring using standard macOS kernel metrics:

  • ✓Full CPU & RAM monitoring: Real-time overall and per-core CPU utilization, memory pressure, and swap usage.
  • ✓Thermals & Fan monitoring: Sensor temperatures and fan RPM readouts.
  • ✓Battery, Network & S.M.A.R.T.: Battery cycle counts, live bandwidth throughput, Wi-Fi latency, and SSD wear.
