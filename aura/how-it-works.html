How Aura rewrites your text with authentic human tone.

Most AI writing tools sound sterile, generic, and painfully recognizable. Aura runs local LLMs directly on your Mac with fine-tuned directives designed to eliminate robotic mannerisms, clichés, and filler, preserving your genuine voice.

Aura is instructed to never use stereotypical AI tropes — phrases like "delve into", "in today's fast-paced world", "testament to", or excessive exclamation points and bullet points. Whether proofreading an email or polishing an essay, Aura sounds like a sharp human editor sitting beside you.
