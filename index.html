Runs 100% on your Mac

Every app is compiled as a lean Universal 2 binary in native Swift & SwiftUI. Inference runs locally on Apple Silicon GPUs and the Neural Engine — no remote API keys, no cloud latency, and no dependency on third-party servers staying online.

Proof: Disconnect your internet completely. Every rewrite, dictation, and scan still works — only the handful of features that are inherently online by nature (like a network speed test) need a connection.

Zero telemetry or tracking

Not a single analytics or crash-reporting SDK is linked into our code — no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Mixpanel, and no Sentry telemetry. We don't know who you are, what hardware you own, or what you type.

Proof: Inspect binaries with strings /Applications/AppName.app/Contents/MacOS/* | grep -i analytics — zero telemetry libraries.

Buy once, own it forever

Software is a tool, not a landlord. When you purchase an app, your license is verified locally via Apple receipt or offline key. There are no monthly subscriptions, no recurring surprise charges, and no remote kill-switches.

Proof: 1-time purchases with generous permanent free tiers. All future updates for the major version are included.

Before you buy — honest hardware & technical boundaries

We believe trust is earned through complete technical honesty before purchase, not after.

● Vitals Compatibility

Runs on macOS 13+ (Ventura, Sonoma, Sequoia, Tahoe). Works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Note: Apple Silicon's true performance cluster separation (hw.perflevelN) and thermal throttling attribution require M-series chips; Intel Macs report overall core usage via standard sysctl.

● Breeze File Safety

Breeze moves files to the macOS Trash first so you can restore anything you ever need; it never permanently deletes behind your back unless you use The Shredder. System Integrity Protection (SIP) protects critical Apple files from any third-party cleaner.

● StealthShare Scope

Does not require Screen Recording permission — it masks windows using native on-screen frosted overlay panels positioned via Core Graphics window coordinates. It masks designated background apps and desktop items, but cannot censor sensitive information or tabs inside an unmasked active window you choose to share.

● Aura & WhisperType AI Requirements

AI models require Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) for Neural Engine & GPU acceleration. Initial model downloads require Wi-Fi once (75 MB for Whisper Tiny up to 3.9 GB for Aura Writer). Aura's largest models (Writer, Best, Expert) recommend 16 GB to 24 GB+ unified memory.
