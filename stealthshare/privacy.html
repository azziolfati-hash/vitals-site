Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: StealthShare collects nothing and never records your screen. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours in the loop. It hides things on your Mac while you share, and puts them all back when you're done. This page explains exactly what that means.

What StealthShare does — and doesn't — see

StealthShare is a curtain, not a camera. It does not record, capture, screenshot, or stream your display, ever. To hide the right things at the right time, it reads a small amount of local system state through standard macOS APIs — the list of apps that are currently running (to detect calls and know which windows to mask), and, on the direct-download build, whether any app is actively using the camera or microphone. None of this is stored or sent anywhere; it's used in the moment and discarded.

What it changes while you share — all temporary

  • ✓Masks the confidential app windows you choose (Slack, Messages, Mail, and any you add) with an on-screen cover — visible to your audience, still readable to you.
  • ✓Hides desktop icons and Mac widgets, swaps in a neutral wallpaper, and turns on a Focus so notification banners are held.
  • ✓Every one of these is reversed the moment Privacy Mode turns off — and StealthShare also restores everything if it quits, so nothing is left changed.

Your calendar (only if you turn Calendar Auto-Arm on)

If you enable Calendar Auto-Arm, StealthShare requests read-only access to your macOS Calendar so it can arm Privacy Mode shortly before a scheduled Zoom, Teams or Meet event. It reads event times and the meeting link only, entirely on your Mac, to decide when to switch on — it never modifies your calendar and never uploads or transmits your events. If you leave the feature off, StealthShare doesn't touch your calendar at all.

What's stored, and where

  • ✓Your settings (which protections are on, your app-blur list, the hotkey) are kept in standard macOS app preferences, local to your Mac.
  • ✓There is no history file, no log of your calls, and no record of what was on your screen — StealthShare has nothing like that to keep.
  • ✓Your purchase is validated through the App Store / StoreKit under your own Apple ID. StealthShare stores no payment details.

The only two things that ever touch the network

The "More Apps" panel

The More Apps section loads a single web page from hanncrest.com that lists our other Mac apps. It's a read-only page — opening it sends no personal data, and tapping an app just opens its page in your browser.

"Report a Bug" — only when you send one

If you choose to file a bug report, StealthShare sends only what you can see and approve first: your message, the app and macOS version, and — only if you leave the optional diagnostics toggle on — an anonymous hardware profile (Mac model, CPU/RAM, locale, uptime, thermal state). It contains no name, account, files, location, or any identifier that ties two reports together. Nothing is ever sent in the background; a report leaves your Mac only when you tap Send Report, and you can inspect the exact contents beforehand.

The permissions StealthShare asks for

To do its job, StealthShare may ask for Screen Recording and Accessibility permission — these let it position the masks over the right windows; despite the name, "Screen Recording" access is what macOS requires to read window layout, and StealthShare uses it only for that, never to record. It also uses macOS Shortcuts to toggle a Focus for Do Not Disturb, and (optionally) read-only Calendar access as described above. All are local macOS permissions you grant in System Settings; none of them send anything anywhere.

Children's privacy

StealthShare is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — there's simply nothing collected, full stop.
