Common questions

Does StealthShare record my screen?

No — never. StealthShare is the opposite of a recorder: it hides things while you share and captures nothing. It doesn't screenshot, record, or stream your display, and it keeps no log of your calls or what was on screen. Everything it does happens locally and is undone when you're finished.

What's free, and what does it cost?

Every feature is unlocked free for 14 days — no account or card needed to start. After the trial, StealthShare is a single one-time purchase of $9.99 that keeps it (and all future updates) forever. There's no subscription.

Why does it ask for Screen Recording permission if it never records?

macOS puts the ability to read window positions and titles behind the "Screen Recording" permission — that's what StealthShare uses it for, so it can place its masks over the exact windows you asked it to hide. It reads layout only, and records nothing. You grant this under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then relaunch StealthShare.

How does window masking work — will my audience see my Slack?

When Privacy Mode is on, StealthShare covers each app you've chosen (Slack, Messages, Mail, and any you add) with an on-screen mask. Your audience sees the mask; you still see the window normally, so you can keep working. The masks lift the instant you turn Privacy Mode off.

Can it turn on automatically when a call starts?

Yes. StealthShare can auto-arm the moment Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, a Slack huddle or FaceTime becomes active, and the direct-download build can also watch for any app using your camera or mic. Turn on Calendar Auto-Arm and it will even arm about 60 seconds before a scheduled meeting on your calendar.

My Do Not Disturb / wallpaper didn't change — what's missing?

Do Not Disturb is driven through macOS Shortcuts: create the two Focus shortcuts StealthShare names in Settings and it will run them automatically. Wallpaper and window masking need Screen Recording and Accessibility permission granted in System Settings; after granting them, relaunch StealthShare so it can take effect.

I bought StealthShare on another Mac — how do I restore it?

If you bought through the Mac App Store, open StealthShare and choose Restore Purchases — it restores from the same Apple ID at no extra charge.

Why is the "detect any app using camera or mic" option missing?

That catch-all watch needs access the Mac App Store sandbox doesn't allow, so it's available in the direct-download build only. In the App Store version, meeting detection still covers Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack huddles, FaceTime and any apps you add by name.
