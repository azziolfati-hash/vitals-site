Everything that could leak, handled

One switch. Your whole screen, made safe.

Screen sharing exposes far more than the tab you meant to show — private chats, desktop clutter, a message banner at the worst moment. StealthShare closes every one of those gaps at once.

🫥

Confidential window masking

Slack, Messages, Mail, WhatsApp and any app you add are blurred in the shared feed — but stay perfectly readable to you. Nothing to close, nothing to move.

🖥️

Desktop & widget hiding

Tucks away every desktop icon and Mac widget behind a clean surface, so a messy desktop or a revealing filename never shows up mid-call.

🖼️

Studio backdrop

Swaps in a neutral, professional wallpaper the moment you go live — and puts your own back the instant you're done.

🔕

Notification blocker

Holds banners while you share so no message preview pops up on screen. After the call it tells you how long they were held and which comms apps to check.

🎥

Meeting auto-detect

Arms itself the moment Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, a Slack huddle or FaceTime goes live — and can catch any app using your camera or mic.

📅

Calendar auto-arm

Reads your calendar (locally, read-only) and turns Privacy Mode on about 60 seconds before a scheduled Zoom / Teams / Meet call — you're protected before you even join.

Nothing permanent, ever

Arm → Share → Restore.

StealthShare never captures your screen and never changes anything for good. Everything it does is a temporary curtain that drops the moment you're finished.

STEP 1

Arm

Press ⌘⇧P, flip the switch, or let a call arm it for you. Your chosen protections snap on together in an instant.

STEP 2

Share

Present with confidence. Confidential windows are masked, the desktop is clean, banners are held and the wallpaper is neutral.

STEP 3

Restore

End Privacy Mode and everything reverts — icons, widgets, wallpaper and Focus back exactly as they were, with a note on any held notifications.
